# Acoustic drum recordings

Pearl Master Studio Pack 1 by **enoe**, distributed by Oramics / Sampled.
Source: https://oramics.github.io/sampled/DRUMS/pearl-master-studio/
License: Creative Commons Attribution 3.0 Unported, https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/3.0/

The five WAV files are distributed unchanged. KINS selects a recording by drum type and closest measured body resonance, adjusts playback speed to the selected Hz, and applies an amplitude envelope. No synthesized fallback is used.

Measurements: stereo channels averaged, Hann-window FFT with 131072 points at the native 44100 Hz. Tom and snare resonances measured over 30–300 ms; kick over 100 ms to end. Dominant body resonances: tom-01 196.49 Hz, tom-02 150.40 Hz, tom-03 72.67 Hz, kick-01 60.90 Hz, snare-01 497.28 Hz.

Acoustic drum recordings contain inharmonic modes, noise and pitch decay. The selected Hz shifts the measured resonance; the recording is an approximate listening reference, not a pure calibration tone or a prediction of a real drum's lug-to-fundamental relationship.
