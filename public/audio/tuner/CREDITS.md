KINS distributes the selected MP3 files unchanged. Playback selects the closest recorded note, adjusts playback speed to the requested frequency using measured sample pitch, and applies a short amplitude envelope. No oscillator or synthesized fallback is used. Calibration measurements were made from the recordings' sustained sections; natural attack/decay pitch variation remains.

The measurements manifest records all candidates audited during development. The Cs1 bass sample was excluded because of excessive pitch drift.
