THE STORY

About The Kins

Concise overview tailored for festival booklets and venue promotional flyers.

Formed in the heart of Melbourne's bustling live music scene, The Kins are a four-piece indie rock outfit known for explosive stage energy, infectious guitar riffs, and setlists that keep pub crowds singing until last call.

Combining the post-punk urgency of classic 80s indie with modern alt-rock grit, The Kins have quickly established themselves as a reliable draw across Melbourne's premier live venues—including sold-out nights at The Espy, The Tote, and Cherry Bar. Whether headlining weekend pub slots or driving festival mainstages, the band delivers a seamless blend of original anthems and re-energized crowdspleasing covers.

Key Milestones & Pitch Highlights

  • Consistent Local Draw: Average 200+ ticket sales in Melbourne inner-north venues.
  • Flexible Set Durations: 30-min opening sets to 90-min multi-set pub headliners.
  • Production Ready: In-ear monitors (IEMs), digital input snake, clean stage footprint.
  • Active Marketing: Dedicated social promo campaign for every booked gig date.
